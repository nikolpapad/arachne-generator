How to use
  • The best guess is already drawn as a box. If it fits, press its kind 1–4.
  • If it doesn't fit: drag a corner to resize, drag the inside to move, or drag on an empty spot to draw a new one.
  • Grey dashed boxes are other guesses: click one to use it too.
  • Several figures? Give each its own box. Tab switches box.
  • Only box humans. A guess on something else (animal, vase…): remove it with Del.
  • No usable figure (too small, tangled with other motifs): press x.
  • t shrinks the box to the stitches inside. ← goes back.
